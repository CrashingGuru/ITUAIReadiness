OECD AI Policy Observatory - Summary

1. OVERVIEW
The OECD AI Policy Observatory (OECD.AI) provides data and multidisciplinary analysis on AI policy. It monitors AI policies across OECD member countries and partner economies.

2. KEY POLICY AREAS TRACKED
- AI national strategies and action plans
- AI governance and regulation frameworks
- AI investment (public and private)
- AI skills and workforce development
- AI research and innovation ecosystem
- Trustworthy AI principles implementation
- AI and the labor market
- AI in the public sector

3. AI PRINCIPLES (OECD AI Principles, 2019)
- Inclusive growth, sustainable development, and well-being
- Human-centered values and fairness
- Transparency and explainability
- Robustness, security, and safety
- Accountability

4. METRICS AND INDICATORS TRACKED
- Government AI strategies: 60+ countries have published national AI strategies
- AI R&D spending as percentage of GDP
- AI patent filings by country
- AI startup funding and venture capital
- AI skills penetration in workforce
- AI publications and citations
- Private sector AI adoption rates

5. RELEVANCE TO ITU AI READINESS FRAMEWORK
- OECD policy tracking maps to ITU Dimension 10 (AI & Policies) and Dimension 7 (Strategy Alignment)
- OECD investment metrics map to the Innovation Ecosystem in the Basic Framework
- OECD AI skills data maps to ITU Dimension 9 (Human Impact)
- OECD national strategy tracking provides benchmark data for country assessments
- OECD trustworthy AI principles align with ITU's standards factor
