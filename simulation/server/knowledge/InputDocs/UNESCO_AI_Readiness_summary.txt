UNESCO AI Readiness Assessment Methodology - Summary

1. OVERVIEW
UNESCO's AI Readiness Assessment Methodology (RAM) helps countries evaluate their preparedness for AI adoption. It is part of UNESCO's broader Recommendation on the Ethics of AI (2021).

2. KEY DIMENSIONS ASSESSED
UNESCO RAM evaluates countries across several pillars:
- Legal and regulatory frameworks for AI
- Technology infrastructure and capacity
- Human resources and education
- Economy and innovation ecosystem
- Social and cultural readiness
- Data governance and availability
- Ethical frameworks and human rights considerations

3. ASSESSMENT APPROACH
- Uses a combination of quantitative indicators and qualitative expert assessments
- Employs surveys, interviews, and desk research
- Results in a multi-dimensional readiness profile
- Provides actionable recommendations per dimension

4. RELEVANCE TO ITU AI READINESS FRAMEWORK
- Complementary to ITU's bottom-up approach (ITU derives from use cases; UNESCO takes top-down policy view)
- UNESCO's data governance dimension maps to ITU's Dimension 1 (Data/Model Marketplace)
- UNESCO's ethical frameworks map to ITU's Dimension 10 (AI & Policies)
- UNESCO's human resources dimension maps to ITU's Dimension 9 (Human Impact)
- UNESCO's infrastructure dimension maps to ITU's Dimension 13 (Digital Infrastructure)

5. COUNTRIES ASSESSED
UNESCO has conducted RAM assessments in multiple developing countries, providing country-level baseline data that could be used as input for the ITU AI Readiness Toolkit.
