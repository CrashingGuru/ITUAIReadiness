ITU AI for Good Sandbox Network - Summary

1. OVERVIEW
The ITU AI for Good Sandbox is a distributed, standards-based platform that lowers barriers to AI research, experimentation, and innovation. It provides cloud computing resources, datasets, and tools for AI experimentation aligned with ITU-T standards.

2. KEY CAPABILITIES
- Cloud credit support for AI experimentation projects
- Access to computing resources (GPU/CPU) for model training
- Hosting environment for AI models and datasets
- Sandbox environments for testing AI solutions in controlled settings
- Integration with ITU-T standards for interoperability

3. SANDBOX NETWORK VISION
- Distributed network of sandboxes across regions and countries
- Standards-based (ITU-T Y.3172) pipeline architecture
- Enables collaboration across countries and domains
- Bridges gap between open science, research, and standardization
- Local deployment capability for data sovereignty

4. USE CASES SUPPORTED
- AI model training and validation
- Policy impact simulation and extrapolation
- Domain-specific AI testing (healthcare, agriculture, disaster management)
- Cross-domain correlation analysis
- GenAI content generation and evaluation

5. RELEVANCE TO ITU AI READINESS FRAMEWORK
- The Sandbox is the primary hosting environment for the AI-RE Toolkit
- Dimension 10 (AI & Policies): Sandbox provides simulated timelines for policy what-if analysis
- Dimension 11 (AI for Inclusion): Sandbox hosts digital twins and simulation environments
- Factor 6 (Sandbox): Directly maps to the sandbox environments factor
- Knowledge Base is hosted within the Sandbox infrastructure
- Multiple toolkit instances can run in parallel, connected to the Sandbox for KB record transfer

6. PLUGFEST INTEGRATION
- Cloud credits provided to selected Plugfest projects
- Projects validated in sandbox environments
- Results feed back into the AI Readiness Framework dimensions and metrics
