Oxford Insights Government AI Readiness Index 2024 - Summary

1. OVERVIEW
The Government AI Readiness Index, published annually by Oxford Insights, ranks countries on their preparedness to implement AI in public services. The 2024 edition covers 193 countries.

2. PILLARS OF ASSESSMENT
The index evaluates three main pillars:
a) Government Pillar:
   - Vision and governance of AI
   - Digital capacity and adaptability of government
   - Data availability and representativeness
b) Technology Sector Pillar:
   - Size and maturity of the technology sector
   - Human capital and innovation capacity
   - Technology infrastructure
c) Data and Infrastructure Pillar:
   - Data availability and quality
   - Infrastructure for AI deployment
   - Representativeness of data

3. TOP RANKED COUNTRIES (2024)
- United States, Singapore, United Kingdom, Finland, Canada, South Korea, Australia, France, Japan, Germany

4. KEY FINDINGS
- Growing gap between AI leaders and developing economies
- Data governance remains a critical differentiator
- Open-source AI adoption accelerating globally
- National AI strategies increasingly common but implementation varies widely
- Digital infrastructure remains the biggest barrier for developing economies

5. RELEVANCE TO ITU AI READINESS FRAMEWORK
- Oxford pillar structure maps to multiple ITU dimensions
- Government pillar → ITU Dimensions 7 (Strategy), 10 (Policies)
- Technology pillar → ITU Dimensions 5 (AI Integration), 9 (Human Impact), 13 (Infrastructure)
- Data pillar → ITU Dimensions 1 (Data Marketplace), 4 (Contextualization)
- Country rankings provide baseline benchmarks for simulation game scenarios
- Developing country scores useful as starting points for delegate inputs
