Kenya National AI Strategy 2025-2030 - Summary

1. OVERVIEW
Kenya's National AI Strategy was launched in March 2025, developed by the Ministry of Information, Communications and the Digital Economy. It provides a comprehensive outlook for strengthening Kenya's AI ecosystem across economic, social, and political dimensions.

2. STRATEGIC PILLARS
- AI Governance and Ethics: Establishing frameworks for responsible AI use
- AI Infrastructure: Building computing and connectivity infrastructure
- AI Talent Development: Expanding AI education and skills training
- AI Research and Innovation: Strengthening R&D capacity
- AI Adoption: Promoting AI uptake across sectors (agriculture, health, finance, education)
- Data Ecosystem: Improving data availability, quality, and governance

3. KEY TARGETS
- Position Kenya as an AI hub in East Africa
- Train 100,000+ AI practitioners by 2030
- Establish AI centers of excellence in universities
- Create regulatory sandboxes for AI experimentation
- Develop local language AI models (Swahili, other Kenyan languages)

4. PRIORITY SECTORS
- Agriculture: Precision farming, crop disease detection, weather prediction
- Healthcare: Diagnostic AI, drug discovery, health system optimization
- Financial Services: Fraud detection, credit scoring, mobile money AI
- Education: Personalized learning, automated assessment
- Government Services: Smart governance, citizen service delivery

5. INFRASTRUCTURE PLANS
- National data center expansion (Konza Technopolis)
- Cloud computing partnerships
- Open data initiatives through Kenya Open Data Portal
- 5G rollout for AI-ready connectivity

6. RELEVANCE TO ITU AI READINESS FRAMEWORK
- Strong alignment with Dimension 7 (Strategy Alignment) — clear top-down national strategy
- Dimension 4 (Contextualization) — emphasis on local language models and local data
- Dimension 9 (Human Impact) — ambitious talent development targets
- Dimension 13 (Digital Infrastructure) — infrastructure investment plans
- Dimension 12 (Granular Priorities) — clear sector-specific priorities
- Kenya participates in ITU AI for Good initiatives and Plugfest projects
